A 60 second voice check for memory health

Speak for about one minute. The check listens to the quality of your voice, not to what you say, and tells you whether a simple follow up test is worth doing.

  • Takes about two minutes in total.
  • Works with any phone or computer microphone.
  • In this prototype your recording never leaves your device.

Please talk for about one minute

A suggestion of what to talk about Describe what you did yesterday, from waking up until lunch. There are no right or wrong answers, and it does not need to be interesting.
The microphone is not available, so this prototype is simulating a recording. Nothing is being captured.

Recording. Speak in your normal voice.

You can stop early once you have spoken for at least 20 seconds. The check works best with the full minute.

Listening to your voice

This takes a few seconds. The check measures how the voice sounds, not the words.

  1. Checking the recording quality
  2. Measuring voice steadiness (pitch and loudness stability)
  3. Measuring voice clarity (breathiness and hoarseness)
  4. Measuring speech rhythm (pauses and pace)
  5. Comparing with the reference range for your age group

    Where the voice check fits

    Speech is not the diagnosis. It is the cheap first step that decides who needs the expensive tests, so that early diagnosis becomes affordable for everyone over 55.

    Step 0
    60 second voice check Any phone, near zero cost, everyone 55 and over.
    Step 1
    Blood test (plasma p-Tau217) Only for those flagged by voice. Targeted and much cheaper than imaging.
    Step 2
    PET scan or specialist Reserved for the few with a positive blood test.
    Step 3
    Treatment in the window New disease modifying drugs work best when started early.

    What the evidence looks like so far

    A weekend proof of concept on public speech data, evaluated on a locked test set that was opened once. The numbers are modest and reported as they are.

    0.62AUC on the locked test set, speaker level split (0.5 is chance)
    344clips from 171 speakers, real spontaneous speech
    Voice qualitycarries the signal. Pause timing is weak, filled pauses add nothing

    Two tempting results were rejected on the way: a flashier model that overfit (0.61 in cross validation, 0.42 on the test set) and an early detection curve that did not hold up. Full code, tables and limitations are in the GitHub repository.
