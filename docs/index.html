How this was computed

Speech is separated from silence at 35 dB below the recording's peak, matching librosa.effects.split(top_db=35) in the pipeline, with 128 ms windows at a 32 ms hop. Gaps between speech runs are pauses, split at 180 ms after He et al. (2025). Rates divide by voiced seconds, not clip length — clip length was a collection artefact and is excluded from the model.

The server adds 88 eGeMAPS voice-quality features via openSMILE, standardises all 96 against the training data, and passes them to the SVM chosen in the study. The result is where that score falls among the development-set recordings — a percentile, not a probability. The model was fitted without probability calibration, and inventing one afterwards would be a number nobody validated.

If you are worried about your memory, talk to your doctor. This is a research prototype and not a medical device. It does not diagnose and must not be used to make health decisions on its own.

What the evidence supports

A weekend proof of concept on 344 clips from 171 speakers of public interview audio. Split by speaker, so nobody appears in both training and test. The locked test set was opened once.

The highlighted row is the model this product runs. Voice quality carries the signal; pause timing alone is barely above chance, which is why the on-device measurement is shown as a measurement and the recommendation comes only from the full model.

Why a yes/no and not a percentage

At AUC 0.62 a personal probability would be noise with a decimal point. Screening tools that work — mammography recall, PSA thresholds — report an action and its error rate, not a likelihood. The line on the result is a product decision: it flags the top third of the development-set distribution. It is not a validated clinical cut-off, and the page says so.

Two results we threw away

A better-looking model that was cheating

Gradient boosting led in cross-validation at 0.605, then scored 0.417 on the locked test set — worse than chance. It had memorised individual speakers. Dropped.

An early-detection curve that was not real

An earlier model showed signal rising through the decade before diagnosis — exactly the story a pitch wants. Under the model that generalises it flattened: 0.577 at 0–5 years, 0.596 at 5–10, 0.405 at 10–15. No longer claimed.

Known limitations

  • 0.62 AUC is a proof of concept. Published systems using controlled speech tasks reach 0.77–0.93. That gap is the roadmap.
  • Celebrity interview audio is noisy and edited. Diagnoses are public record, not biomarker-confirmed.
  • English-dominant, with no fairness testing across demographics yet.
  • Not a medical device. No regulatory clearance of any kind.
